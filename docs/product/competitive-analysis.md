Purpose

Define where you will compete and where you won't.

Suggested Competitors

Use a table:

Product	Strengths	Weaknesses
Jira	Engineering workflows	Weak portfolio reporting
Monday.com	Easy UI	Limited governance
ClickUp	Feature-rich	Complex and noisy
Microsoft Project	Scheduling	Outdated UX
Smartsheet	Spreadsheet familiarity	Limited PMO governance

Your Positioning

Something like:
Not another task manager.

A self-hosted PMO and Portfolio Management platform
with built-in governance, executive reporting,
project health and AI assistance.

This positioning is much stronger than competing head-on with task management tools.

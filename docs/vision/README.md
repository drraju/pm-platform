# Vision Documentation

The **Vision** documentation defines the long-term direction of the PM Platform.

These documents answer **why** the platform exists, what problems it solves, and the principles that guide strategic decisions.

---

## Documents

### PRODUCT_VISION.md

Defines the long-term vision, mission, platform scope, guiding principles and strategic objectives.

---

### PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md

Describes market positioning, product strategy, roadmap themes and long-term business direction.

---

### PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md

Defines the architectural and engineering principles that govern every major design decision.

---

### DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md

Defines the user experience philosophy, interface consistency, accessibility and design standards.

---

## Intended Audience

* Product Managers
* Architects
* Engineering Leadership
* Executive Stakeholders

---

## Reading Order

1. PRODUCT_VISION.md
2. PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md
3. PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md
4. DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md

These documents rarely change and represent the long-term direction of the platform.

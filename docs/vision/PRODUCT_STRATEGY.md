# PM Platform – Product Strategy

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Strategic Goal

To become an enterprise delivery platform that unifies project management, portfolio management, resource management, customer success, governance and AI-assisted decision support within a single integrated solution.

---

# Target Customers

Primary:

* Mid-size enterprises
* Large enterprises
* Managed Service Providers
* Technology organizations
* Consulting firms
* Professional Services organizations

Secondary:

* PMOs
* Delivery organizations
* Customer Success teams
* Operations teams

---

# Strategic Differentiators

The platform differentiates itself through:

* Unified project and customer success management
* Enterprise governance
* AI-assisted delivery
* Provider-agnostic integrations
* Modular architecture
* Strong documentation and engineering governance

---

# Strategic Objectives

## Improve Delivery Predictability

Enable organizations to plan and deliver projects with greater confidence.

---

## Improve Executive Visibility

Provide accurate, real-time insights across portfolios, programs and projects.

---

## Improve Resource Utilization

Support effective planning and optimisation of people, skills and capacity.

---

## Improve Customer Outcomes

Connect project execution with customer success.

---

## Reduce Operational Complexity

Replace disconnected spreadsheets and point solutions with an integrated platform.

---

## AI-Augmented Decision Making

Use AI to assist rather than replace human decision making.

---

# Product Positioning

PM Platform is positioned as an Enterprise Delivery Management Platform rather than a traditional task management application.

It combines:

* Project Management
* Portfolio Management
* Resource Management
* Customer Success
* Governance
* AI

within a unified architecture.

---

# Strategic Principles

The platform will:

* Prefer integration over duplication.
* Prefer governance over unrestricted flexibility.
* Prefer long-term maintainability over short-term speed.
* Prefer enterprise scalability over niche optimisation.
* Deliver incremental value through continuous improvement.
